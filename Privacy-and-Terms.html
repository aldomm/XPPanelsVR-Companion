Mac local processing

ScreenCaptureKit captures recognized simulator windows after permission and Start Sharing. Outside video is separately enabled. Local popup-position telemetry identifies instruments. Approved LAN clients can receive these images. The optional local preview captures the selected whole display. Version 2.0 (3) optionally shares X-Plane application audio with one approved client; it stops when disabled or disconnected. No microphone is captured and audio is not saved. The retained 0.1.2 package does not provide audio streaming.

Optional Vision Pro ISCS sharing/input lets an approved headset view the selected simulator window and send clicks using Accessibility. Permission is off by default and session-only. Clicks move the real desktop pointer. Image tickets expire after 1.5 seconds, are single-use and address-bound; source-window and uncovered-target checks apply. Stop Sharing revokes access. These controls are not encrypted or cryptographically authenticated pairing.

Open Missing Displays sends the supported aircraft's instrument-popup commands locally when you request it. Optional Accessibility permission allows sizing and moving identified simulator instrument windows to your selected Mac display, including checked native-title-bar and resize-corner mouse drags when X-Plane does not expose movable accessibility windows. It is not required for capture, the preview or popup opening. The app does not record keystrokes or use Accessibility to inspect other applications. You may revoke this permission in macOS Privacy settings.

Private-network use

Companion is designed for a direct connection between your Mac and headset on a trusted private network. Your simulator stream is not routed through an AldoApps cloud service. Sharing starts off; you start it and approve video viewers for each session. Stopping sharing revokes those approvals.

The current HTTP video and UDP simulator protocols do not provide transport encryption. Use a network whose devices and access you trust, protect Wi-Fi with a strong password, and do not forward these ports to the internet. Private-network use limits exposure but does not remove all security risks. Address approval is not cryptographic authentication, and it does not protect the simulator's command ports. Public, hotel and other shared networks are not suitable for this setup.

Settings, plugin receipts and recovery backups stay on the Mac. Bridge logs remain in the user's Library/Logs directory and can contain window titles, IDs and technical errors. The diagnostic report is saved only where you choose; review it before emailing support. No analytics, advertising, automatic diagnostic upload or publisher account is included.

Delete the application to stop using it; use Settings to unregister login startup and remove the plugin first. Saved settings, logs and backups may remain until you delete them. Emailing support sends your message and attachments to the support mailbox provider; do not include sensitive data unnecessarily.
